Delete Your CrateLuv Account

You can delete your CrateLuv account and its data at any time.

Option 1: Delete in the app

  1. Open CrateLuv and sign in.
  2. Open the menu and choose Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email support@crateluv.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my CrateLuv account". We'll verify the request and delete the account, normally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, phone number, rescue instructions, saved crate location, and settings)
  • Your rescue contacts
  • Your timers and activity log
  • Your push notification tokens

What we keep

To prevent repeated use of the free trial, we keep a minimal record after deletion: your email address and the date your free trial began. No other account data is kept for this purpose. We may also retain information where required by law or to resolve disputes, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel it separately in your Google Play (or Apple ID) subscription settings so you are not billed again.
