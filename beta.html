How it works

  1. Add your emergency contacts. Enter email addresses and phone numbers for the people who would help your pets. CrateLuv contacts each one and asks if they're okay being an emergency contact. Once they accept, they join your list.
  2. Set your crate's location. When you get home, CrateLuv can stop the timer automatically so no one gets notified by mistake. (Automatic stop is part of your free month of all features.)
  3. Start a timer for how long you'll be gone. For testing there's a 3-minute timer so you can watch it work, and you can also set custom times.
  4. If the timer runs out, we reach you first, by text (if you've allowed it), email and push notification. If you don't respond, after 15 minutes (the default) we try your first contact, then your next one 15 minutes later, and so on until someone responds or the list runs out. You choose the order and the wait between contacts.

Once a timer is running, the alerts are handled in the cloud, so they still go out if your phone dies.

Why we built it

CrateLuv was inspired by the tragic events that befell Gene Hackman in Santa Fe. They had left one of their dogs penned up, and the poor dog died because no one knew. Gene and his wife died as well. After reading that news, we were heading out to dinner and had just put our two Boston terriers in their crates (their safe place, and they love them). I started thinking: what if we're in a wreck? What happens to the pups?

That's how CrateLuv was born.

What to expect during the beta

Your free month

Everyone gets all features free for 30 days from the day you sign up. After that, the app moves to the free plan, and the paid features need a subscription. The free trial is per person and does not restart if you delete and re-create your account.

Free plan vs. paid

  • Paid features: crate geofence auto-stop, custom and 1-hour timers, up to 5 rescue contacts, SMS contacts, unlimited timer starts, and a second account owner.
  • Free plan: 2/3/4-hour timers, 1 rescue contact (email), manual stop, and 3 timer starts per week.
  • No weekly timer limit during your free month.

Subscriptions during the beta

You don't need to subscribe to try everything: your first 30 days include all features. If you do want to test the subscription screens, iPhone beta purchases run in Apple's sandbox, so you won't be charged, and test subscriptions renew in minutes and then stop. Android purchases are only free if we've added your Google account as a license tester. Email us first and we'll add you, otherwise please don't subscribe during the beta.

Testing shortcuts

The main screen offers a 3 min timer so you can quickly watch a timer expire and see the alerts arrive. It is for beta testing only and will be removed for the public release; real use should be hours. The standard grace period is 15 or 30 minutes, so a full contact cascade takes a while even with a 3-minute timer.

Things to know

  • Keep the app updated. We may require an update to keep using the beta if we fix something important.
  • Allow notifications and location (choose "Always" for geofence auto-stop) when prompted.
  • Data may be reset. Accounts and timers can be wiped between beta builds.
  • Your contacts will get real emails and texts when a timer expires, so tell them a test may be coming.

Send feedback

Bugs, confusing screens, missed alerts: email support@crateluv.com, or use the button on the app's About screen (it includes your app version automatically). Tell us your phone model and what you expected to happen.
