CrateLuv Beta: What to Expect

Thank you for helping test CrateLuv. This page explains how the beta works, what's different from the finished app, and how to send feedback.

Please don't rely on the beta as your only safeguard. CrateLuv is beta software and can fail (missed notifications, phone settings that stop background apps, no signal, and bugs we haven't found yet). Only crate a pet you would normally crate anyway, and keep your usual precautions in place. See the Terms of Use.

Your free month

Everyone gets all features free for 30 days from the day you sign up. After that, the app moves to the free plan, and the paid features need a subscription. The free trial is per person and does not restart if you delete and re-create your account.

Free plan vs. paid

  • Paid features: crate geofence auto-stop, custom and 1-hour timers, up to 5 rescue contacts, SMS contacts, unlimited timer starts, and a second account owner.
  • Free plan: 2/3/4-hour timers, 1 rescue contact (email), manual stop, and 3 timer starts per week.
  • No weekly timer limit during the beta.

Purchases are in test mode

During the beta, subscriptions run in the App Store / Google Play sandbox. You will not be charged real money. Sandbox subscriptions renew much faster than real ones (minutes instead of months) and then stop, so a test subscription can expire while you're using it, which is expected. You'll need a sandbox/test account: Apple testers use a Sandbox Apple ID; Android testers must be on the license-testing list. We'll tell you if we need to add you.

Testing shortcuts

During the beta, the main screen offers a 3 min timer so you can quickly watch a timer expire and see the alerts arrive. It is for beta testing only and will be removed for the public release; real use should be hours. Some test accounts can also shorten the grace period to 2 minutes; the standard grace period is 15 or 30 minutes, so a full contact cascade takes a while even with a 3-minute timer.

Things to know

  • Keep the app updated. We may require an update to keep using the beta if we fix something important.
  • Allow notifications and location (choose "Always" for geofence auto-stop) when prompted.
  • Data may be reset. Accounts and timers can be wiped between beta builds.
  • Your contacts will get real emails and texts when a timer expires, so tell them a test may be coming.

Send feedback

Bugs, confusing screens, missed alerts: email support@crateluv.com, or use the button on the app's About screen (it includes your app version automatically). Tell us your phone model and what you expected to happen.
