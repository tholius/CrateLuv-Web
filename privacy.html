Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CrateLuv is a crate-timer app for pets. To provide its features, we collect:

  • Account information: your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Contact information for your rescue cascade: the names and phone numbers of the contacts you choose to notify if a timer runs past its grace period, solely for delivering those safety alerts.
  • Location data: with your permission, CrateLuv uses your device's location — including in the background, while the app is not open — to detect when you return to a crate location you've saved, so it can automatically stop an active timer. Background location is only used while a timer is running, and only if you've enabled this optional feature. You can disable location permissions at any time in your device settings, which will turn off automatic stop but will not otherwise limit the app.
  • Push notification tokens: a device identifier issued by Apple or Google's notification services, used to deliver timer alerts and rescue-cascade notifications to your device.
  • Diagnostic and crash data: we use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and basic device/app diagnostic information (such as device model and OS version) to help us find and fix bugs. This data is not used for advertising.

2. How We Use This Information

We use the information above only to operate the App's core features: running and displaying your timers, detecting your return to a saved crate location, sending you and your chosen contacts timer and rescue-cascade alerts, and diagnosing technical problems. We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. SMS Privacy (Carrier / 10DLC Requirement)

SMS opt-in data (including mobile numbers and consent you provide for text messaging) is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional purposes. We do not share SMS opt-in information with affiliates or partners for marketing. Information may be processed by service providers (for example, carriers and messaging infrastructure) solely as needed to deliver the program you agreed to, under contractual confidentiality and applicable law—not for those providers to market to you on their own behalf.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. The categories above exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; that opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as required to facilitate message delivery or as compelled by law.

4. Service Providers

We share information with the following categories of service providers, solely to operate the App, and under agreements that limit their use of your data to providing that service to us:

  • Google Firebase — account authentication, app data storage, crash reporting (Crashlytics), and push notification delivery.
  • Carriers and SMS/voice infrastructure providers — delivering the text and voice alerts you and your contacts have opted into.
  • Apple and Google — delivering push notifications to your device via their respective notification services.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account and timer data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account from within the App, we delete your account data within a reasonable time, except where we are required to retain it longer to comply with law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Crash and diagnostic data is retained by Firebase Crashlytics according to Google's standard retention periods.

6. Your Choices

You can disable location permissions or notification permissions at any time in your device settings. You can opt out of SMS at any time as described in Section 3. You can request deletion of your account and associated data from within the App or by contacting us below.

7. Opt-Out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Text STOP to the number messages come from. After you send STOP, we will send a confirmation that you have been unsubscribed.

8. Children's Privacy

CrateLuv is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.
