How to Use CrateLuv

Draft: This page is a first pass and hasn't been fully reviewed yet — some details may change as the app does. This app is a Fremium app, meaning there are free and paid features. For 30 days after you sign up, you will have access to all features. After that, the app will revert to the free version, and requires a subscription to enable the paid features. The paid features are: geofencing, more (and custom) timer lengths, more contacts, and unlimited timer requests (the free version limits how many times you can set a timer each week.) Questions in the meantime: support@crateluv.com.

1. Set your crate location (Paid Feature)

From the main screen, tap Update crate location while you're standing at your dog's crate. This saves your GPS position so CrateLuv can automatically stop your timer once you return, without you having to open the app again. Without this paid feature, you must stop the timer yourself each time you return.

2. Add rescue contacts

Under Contacts, choose the people you want notified if you don't check in before your timer runs out. Each contact needs to confirm their email and/or phone number before they can be notified — you'll see checkmarks once they're approved. You should let them know a request for approval is coming so they will be expecting it. If they mess up and dismiss the request, you can re-send it.

3. Write your rescue instructions

The Rescue Instructions box is what your contacts will see if they're ever notified — for example, where you are, how to get in, and anything they need to know to look after your pet. Keep it short and specific.

4. Start a timer

Pick how long you'll be away using one of the preset durations, or set a custom time (paid feature). Tap Start Timer to begin. If this is your first time (or the Terms of Use have changed since you last agreed), you'll be asked to read and accept them first.

5. Coming home

If you have a crate location saved and geofencing turned on, leaving that area for a couple of minutes and then returning will automatically stop your timer — no need to open the app. You can also stop it manually from the app at any time.

6. If a timer runs out

When a timer expires, you'll have a grace period to confirm you're back before anyone is contacted — tap I'm back, don't contact anyone if that's the case. If you don't respond in time, CrateLuv will start notifying your selected contacts using the rescue instructions you wrote.

Questions?

Email us at support@crateluv.com — you can also send this directly from the app's About screen, which includes your app version automatically.
